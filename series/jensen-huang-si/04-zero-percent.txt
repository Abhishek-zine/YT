You are told to fear superintelligence in 2030. Why does the man selling the chips for it put the risk at 0%?

September 2026, CBS Sunday Morning. Jensen Huang: "2030 is not going to be the end of the world. There is 0% chance."

Eight months earlier, on the No Priors podcast, he was quoted saying: "I guess someday we will have God AI." But he placed it very far in the future, and said no researcher has any reasonable ability to build it today.

So his view is not "never". It is "not soon, so do not slow down".

Two things to remember. Nvidia earns its money selling the chips this race runs on. And in 2023, scientists including Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio signed a statement that extinction risk from AI should be a global priority.

Zero percent is one man's confidence. It is not a measurement.
