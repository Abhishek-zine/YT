You could lose your first job to AI in 5 years, says 1 AI CEO. Why does Nvidia's CEO disagree with almost everything he says?

May 2025. Anthropic's CEO, Dario Amodei, warns that AI could wipe out half of entry-level white-collar jobs within five years.

June 2025, VivaTech in Paris. Jensen Huang says he pretty much disagrees with almost everything Amodei says. Some jobs will disappear, he says, but every job will change. "It's changed mine." His bet: more productive companies end up hiring more people.

Here is the condition nobody quotes. On CNN, Huang added: "If the world runs out of ideas, then productivity gains translate to job loss."

So both men agree your job changes. They disagree on whether new work arrives in time, and nobody has the data to settle it.
