You are planning your life around an AGI date that Nvidia's CEO says has already passed. Why did his answer change in 2 years?

March 2024, Stanford. Jensen Huang is asked when AGI arrives. His answer, as Reuters reported it: if AGI means passing human tests, "I'm guessing in five years we'll do well on every single one."

March 2026, the Lex Fridman podcast. Same man: "I think we've achieved AGI."

Here is the twist. The AI did not jump five years ahead. The definition moved. In 2024 the test was every human exam. In 2026 the test was whether an AI could build a billion dollar company.

So the real question is not when AGI arrives. It is whose definition you are hearing. Before you believe any AGI date, ask that first.
