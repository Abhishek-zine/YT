You are told superintelligence is years away. Why does Nvidia's CEO say it is already on the road?

January 2025, at CES. Jensen Huang says his company is full of people who are superintelligent in their own field. As Engadget reported it: "I'm surrounded by superintelligence."

September 2026, the All-In Summit. According to reports, he points at self-driving cars. In that one narrow job, he argues, the machine is already superintelligent, with a tenth of the human accident rate. That number is his claim, not an independent study.

Now look at what happened to the word. For the philosopher Nick Bostrom, superintelligence is an intellect that greatly exceeds humans in virtually all domains. For Huang, it is beating humans at one thing.

By Huang's meaning, it is here. By Bostrom's, it does not exist yet. Same word, two different things.

Subscribe, and always ask which one you are hearing.
