## 2. How "global, all languages" works on YouTube

- **Audience**: every public video is visible worldwide by default. There is no "worldwide"
  setting to turn on, and you should not add country restrictions.
- **Country (Basic info)**: this is *your* country of residence, used for payments, taxes and
  Partner Program eligibility. It does not limit who sees your videos. Set it to where you
  actually live (India, if that is still true); a false country can get monetization refused.
- **Languages**: one channel that uploads separate videos in ten languages confuses
  recommendations. Instead, make each video once in English and add other languages to it:
  1. **Dubbed audio tracks**: turn on automatic dubbing (section 6), or upload your own dubs
     per video (Studio → video → Languages → Add language → Audio).
  2. **Translated title and description** per video (Studio → video → Languages).
  3. **Subtitles**: upload accurate English captions; viewers can auto-translate them into any
     language.
  4. **Channel name and description translations** (section 3), so the channel page appears in
     the viewer's language.

## 8. Rules for this niche

- **Superintelligence does not exist yet.** Never title or narrate as if it does ("ASI is here!").
  It is clickbait that kills trust and can count as misleading content.
- **Source every prediction**: name the person and the year in the video, and link it in the
  description. `/yt-script` refuses invented numbers, so keep it that way.
- **No deepfakes**: don't use AI-generated faces or voices of real researchers or CEOs.
- **Partner Program**: faceless videos with AI voices are allowed, but YouTube rejects channels for
  "reused content" or "inauthentic content" when videos look mass-produced. Use your own script for
  every video, give each video a clear point of view, and don't publish raw MoneyPrinterTurbo
  output without editing it.
- **Dubs and translations**: check that an automatic dub didn't change a number, name or date
  before it goes public. A wrong figure in one language is still a wrong figure.
