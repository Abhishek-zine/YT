## 6. Content plan

**Content pillars**
1. **Basics**: what AGI and ASI are, how today's AI works, key terms.
2. **Timelines**: what researchers and lab leaders predict, and why they disagree.
3. **Safety and risk**: alignment, control, why some scientists are worried.
4. **Impact on India**: jobs, education, everyday life.

**Format and frequency**
- One long-form video per week (8–12 minutes, 16:9).
- Three Shorts per week (under 60 seconds, 9:16), cut from the long video with `/yt-shorts`.
- Language: Hinglish titles, Hindi narration for most videos, and an English version of the
  strongest ones. Start with **Hindi only for the first 10 videos**: one language is easier for

## 7. Rules for this niche

- **Superintelligence does not exist yet.** Never title or narrate as if it does ("ASI is here!").
  It is clickbait that kills trust and can count as misleading content.
- **Source every prediction**: name the person and the year in the video, and link it in the
  description. `/yt-script` refuses invented numbers, so keep it that way.
- **No deepfakes**: don't use AI-generated faces or voices of real researchers or CEOs.
- **Partner Program**: faceless videos with AI voices are allowed, but YouTube rejects channels for
  "reused content" or "inauthentic content" when videos look mass-produced. Use your own script for
  every video, give each video a clear point of view, and don't publish raw MoneyPrinterTurbo
  output without editing it.
