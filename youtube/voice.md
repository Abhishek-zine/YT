## Who I am talking to

One person: a 25-year-old in the US, UK, Canada or Australia (and English speakers anywhere else)
who uses ChatGPT or Claude every week, keeps hearing "AGI" and "superintelligence" in the news, and
quietly wonders what it means for their job. They are smart but not technical. They watch on a
phone, in English, often with the sound low, so the on-screen text has to carry the point.

## How I actually talk

Plain international English: short sentences, common words, no slang that only works in one
country, no idioms that break in translation or auto-dubbing. Calm and curious, like a friend who
read the transcript so you don't have to, not a news anchor and not a hype channel. Dates and
venues are said out loud ("March 2026, the Lex Fridman podcast").

## Words I never use

"Guys" or "folks" as the opening word, "game-changer", "mind-blowing", "shocking", "insane",
"you won't believe", "dive in", "let's get started", "unlock". No "AI will destroy humanity"
fear-mongering, and no "AI will save us all" cheerleading either.

## Words I do use

"Imagine", "In plain English", "Here is the twist", "Hold on", "The real question is",
"On the record", "Simply explained".

## What I will not claim

- That superintelligence already exists, or a date it will definitely arrive. When someone else
  claims it, I say who, when, and which definition they used.
- Any prediction without naming who said it and when.
- Numbers I can't link to a source.
- Anything a real person "said" that I haven't verified.
- That a named person committed a crime or fraud, unless a court or regulator found it. Accusations
  are reported as accusations, with the person's response.

## My format

Faceless: stock footage, simple diagrams and on-screen text, with a male narrator (US English Edge
TTS `en-US-AndrewNeural`, or a real recording). No intro or channel jingle; open straight on the
hook. Long videos 8–12 minutes, Shorts under 60 seconds. No swearing. One ask at the end: subscribe.

The name stays **Mahabuddhi**. It is Sanskrit for "great intellect", which is the closest old word
for superintelligence. Say that once in the channel trailer; don't explain it in every video.
